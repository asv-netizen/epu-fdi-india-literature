## Abstract

> Abstract We examine the effects of greenfield FDI and cross‐border mergers and acquisitions (M&amp;As) on total factor productivity ( TFP ) in developed and developing host countries of FDI . Using panel data for up to 123 countries over the period from 2003 to 2011, we find that greenfield FDI has no statistically significant effect on TFP , while M&amp;As have a positive effect on TFP in the total sample. Greenfield FDI and M&amp;As both appear to be ineffective in increasing TFP in the subsample of developing countries. In contrast, M&amp;As have a strong and positive effect on TFP in the subsample of developed countries.

Shows the two modes have different real effects. Supports the claim that pooling them hides heterogeneity.
