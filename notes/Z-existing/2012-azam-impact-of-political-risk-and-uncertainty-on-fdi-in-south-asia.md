## Abstract (extracted from the PDF)

> This study examines the potential effect of political risk and macroeconomic policy uncertainty on FDI in South Asia. To highlight the affect of political risk and macro policy uncertainty on FDI, we setup a theoretical framework based on oligopolistic and imperfect competition environment in host country. Autoregressive distributed lags (ARDL) is used to examine the impact of political risk and macro economic policy uncertainty index on FDI inﬂows. Macroeconomic policy uncertainty and political risk indices are constructed for this purpose. The long run results show negative affect of political risk and macroeconomic policy uncertainty indices on FDI inﬂows. Trade openness shows positive effect on FDI inﬂows only in short run while in long run it has negative impact on due lack of creditability regarding consistent trade liberalization policy and high trade cost. Furthermore, themarket size signiﬁcantly affects the inﬂow of FDI both in long run and short run which shows that FDI inﬂows in South Asia are mainly depend on market size. South Asian economies need to focus on political and macroeconomic factors along with FDI incentives policies to attract more FDI.

## Why it matters

South Asia ARDL study with hand-built political-risk and macro-policy-uncertainty indices. The closest regional precedent and a source of control variables.
